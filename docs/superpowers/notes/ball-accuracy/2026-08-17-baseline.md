## Findings — dominant error sources (ranked)

1. **Between-keyframe interpolation is decimetre-to-metre wrong** (the bulk
   of p50). Rolls/carries render constant-speed linear; ballistic spans are
   two-endpoint parabolas through bucket-height endpoints. A held-out touch
   means the path misses the real contact by metres (gberch f371: 6.0 m;
   origi01 f282 airborne header: 24.2 m).
2. **Phantom auto touches inject hard keyframes metres off the path.**
   With zero real detections the stage still minted 24 auto anchors on
   gberch (fold 1), incl. `f170 player_touch P004 l_knee conf=0.94` — 8.4 m
   from GT, on a span where plain linear interpolation lands within ~5 cm.
   Mechanism: anchor pixels (conf 1.0) + appearance-bridge template matches +
   IMM gap-fill form a synthetic pixel track; direction-change segmentation
   finds corners in it; pose_touch attributes any corner to the nearest joint
   within 60 px (high-recall by design, "editor prunes"). Unattended, these
   become body-pinned keyframes that drag the track to the wrong player.
   The naturalness validator cannot flag them (the bend is at an "event").
3. **Airborne keyframes are height-bucket placeholders** (`ray_physics` at
   z = 1.0/6.0/15.0). origi01 airborne_mid/high held-out: 11/11 > 20 cm,
   p50 0.62–1.08 m. The ≥2-hard-knot gravity solve exists only in the unused
   piecewise path.
4. **Touch keyframes can resolve below the pitch** (gberch touches at
   z = −0.08…−0.01; s013 f42 at z = 0.006): FK foot-anchor residual passes
   straight into the ball position at the priority moments.
5. Sparse-anchor clips (kroupi01 12, s013 13 anchors) have long unconstrained
   spans → p50 2.3–4.9 m: they need the detection evidence (A3 path) and
   evidence-constrained rendering far more than anchor-dense clips.

Key W5 findings (each verified by probe, fix committed):
- **False pass-1 clusters** (detector locked on static objects post-kick,
  conf 0.6–0.8) steered evidence rolls 8 m — chord-deviation gate (1.5 m).
- **Junk in-span detections** poisoned chain fits (residuals 50–417 px) —
  two-stage robust fit (anchors-only floor, ≤30 px gated extras, median
  anchor-click acceptance).
- **Auto knots carry attribution error as 3-D error** — soft
  confidence-weighted world constraints (0.5 px/m tiebreak with a manual
  knot; 5 px/m when they're the only depth anchor; measured sweep).
- **The touch contact gate is depth-blind** (a joint near the camera–ball
  LINE passes while metres off along it) — depth-aware attribution scoring
  vs ground-anchor-interpolated expected worlds.
- **The IMM outlier gate rejected operator clicks** (post-kick ball beats
  the innovation gate), freezing the observation track at stale positions
  where static false detections then pass as consistent — anchor frames
  are now exempt and reseed the filter. This one fix moved p95 4.69→3.25
  and cleared the f55/56 cluster. It violated "operator input always wins"
  and had been silently corrupting the shared-core event stream.
- Auto touch **moments** are now found at relaxed recall 6/8 (0.75) vs the
  5/8 union reference; strict (bone-correct) 3/8 — attribution is the gap.
- Remaining tails: f188-class corner-cuts where the bend's event is held
  out AND its auto recovery is suppressed by a nearby kept manual anchor
  of a different state (suppress_radius experiment pending); residual
  naturalness violations are the validator correctly flagging real bends
  whose explaining event is missing from the keyframe set.

**A2 downgraded to a consistency indicator.** The origi01 cross-replay
fixes disagree with the saved track by p50 3.15 m — but a sensitivity
retriangulation shows the fixes themselves move p50 1.52 m (max 8.2 m)
