The calibrated camera is untouched: saved world-space motion is resampled from 30 fps to the model's 20 fps time step, refined in overlapping 16-sample windows, and resampled back. Each contiguous tracking run keeps one starting XY anchor; predicted XY increments are integrated from there, and height is the model's prediction. Runs shorter than the model window are left unchanged.
