# Sub-20 cm Campaign — Baseline Measurements (2026-08-17)

Harness: `scripts/eval_ball_accuracy.py` (spec §4). Hold-out = stratified
alternating 50 % of manual anchors withheld, graded at the withheld frames on
the emitted dense track; both folds run, every anchor held out exactly once.
Errors are 3-D metres where GT exists (`ground_exact` via ray ∩ z=r,
`joint_depth` via contacting joint on the clicked ray − one ball radius,
matching the resolver's convention), else lateral ray distance (a lower
bound). Threshold 0.20 m. Raw reports: `runs/baseline_*.json`.

## Findings — dominant error sources (ranked)

1. **Between-keyframe interpolation is decimetre-to-metre wrong** (the bulk
   of p50). Rolls/carries render constant-speed linear; ballistic spans are
   two-endpoint parabolas through bucket-height endpoints. A held-out touch
   means the path misses the real contact by metres (gberch f371: 6.0 m;
   origi01 f282 airborne header: 24.2 m).
2. **Phantom auto touches inject hard keyframes metres off the path.**
   With zero real detections the stage still minted 24 auto anchors on
   gberch (fold 1), incl. `f170 player_touch P004 l_knee conf=0.94` — 8.4 m
   from GT, on a span where plain linear interpolation lands within ~5 cm.
   Mechanism: anchor pixels (conf 1.0) + appearance-bridge template matches +
   IMM gap-fill form a synthetic pixel track; direction-change segmentation
   finds corners in it; pose_touch attributes any corner to the nearest joint
   within 60 px (high-recall by design, "editor prunes"). Unattended, these
   become body-pinned keyframes that drag the track to the wrong player.
   The naturalness validator cannot flag them (the bend is at an "event").
3. **Airborne keyframes are height-bucket placeholders** (`ray_physics` at
   z = 1.0/6.0/15.0). origi01 airborne_mid/high held-out: 11/11 > 20 cm,
   p50 0.62–1.08 m. The ≥2-hard-knot gravity solve exists only in the unused
   piecewise path.
4. **Touch keyframes can resolve below the pitch** (gberch touches at
   z = −0.08…−0.01; s013 f42 at z = 0.006): FK foot-anchor residual passes
   straight into the ball position at the priority moments.
5. Sparse-anchor clips (kroupi01 12, s013 13 anchors) have long unconstrained
   spans → p50 2.3–4.9 m: they need the detection evidence (A3 path) and
   evidence-constrained rendering far more than anchor-dense clips.

## W2/W3/W4 measured deltas (noop hold-out, p50 | max)

| clip | baseline | after W2 (clamp+gate) | after W3 (+chains, min-move clamp) |
|---|---|---|---|
| gberch | 0.44 / 8.40 | 0.35 / 6.00 | 0.45 / 6.00 |
| origi01 | 0.90 / 24.23 | 0.76 / 24.23 | 0.87 / 24.23 |
| kroupi01 | 2.32 / 7.77 | 3.27 / 7.77 | 3.27 / 7.77 |
| s013 | 4.90 / 12.76 | 5.85 / 13.13 | 5.85 / 12.77 |

Readings:
- The 8.4 m phantom-touch class is gone (gberch max = pure-interp level).
- kroupi01/s013 regress under the gate because on noop hold-out their only
  densification was synthetic-born events, whose helpful members deviate
  3.4–7.7 m from the sparse manual reference — statistically inseparable
  from the 8.6 m phantom (measured). Decision: keep the conservative gate;
  sparse clips' accuracy must come from real detection evidence (W4), which
  is also the operating point (s013 detection coverage 0.99).
- W3 chain refits rarely fire on noop hold-out (bracketing hard knots are
  themselves often held out); their value arrives with W4 detection
  evidence. Synthetic-arc unit tests prove the machinery (≤5 cm on truth).
- origi01 kept-touch 5.03 m regression from the first W2a clamp
  (along-ray slide on a shallow ray) was found by the harness's kept-anchor
  sanity check and fixed with the min-move clamp; kept anchors are back to
  ≤0.36 m (within joint-GT model uncertainty), 0.00 elsewhere.
- Naturalness violations remain 0 throughout.

W4 (evidence-constrained rendering: rolls follow detection ground points,
chain fits consume in-span detections, free-flight renders gravity arcs,
refit keyframes ray-snapped pre-render) is inert on noop runs by design —
its measurement is the cached wasb runs.

## Implications for the workstreams (spec §5–§8)

- W2 splits into **W2a touch contact geometry** (below-ground clamp — finding
  4) and **W2b evidence-gated auto events** (finding 2): touch proposals
  whose local pixel support is synthetic (anchor-interp/bridge/gap-fill, no
  detector hits nearby) must stay editor *suggestions* and never become
  unattended keyframes; body-pins that displace the ball far from the
  evidence-consistent path without detection support get demoted.
- W3 (airborne physics chains) directly addresses finding 3 (and the 24 m
  origi01 tail).
- W4 (evidence-constrained rendering incl. detection-following rolls and
  carry-follows-player) addresses findings 1 and 5.
